Why trust a solo builder

Not a resume — the actual reasoning behind how I build.

Most teams building AI products have a backend engineer, an ML person, and someone who owns the product decisions. On RxBuddy PK, that was all one person. I built the OCR pipeline, wrote the drug-interaction logic myself rather than delegating it to an LLM, made the call to stay on free-tier infrastructure, and I'm the one currently rebuilding the retrieval layer because the hardcoded dictionary doesn't scale.

That's the actual argument for hiring or working with a solo builder: not that I can do everything perfectly, but that I've already made — and lived with — the real tradeoffs a small team has to make under real constraints. I'm currently going deeper into the parts of this I taught myself along the way, through an ML internship focused on validation, leakage, and honest evaluation — the things that are easy to get wrong quietly.
